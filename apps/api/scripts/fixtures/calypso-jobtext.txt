Introduction
Calypso Ventures GmbH is an AI startup based in Berlin and Regensburg, founded by two Oxford graduates. We build AI Portfolio optimization software for global commodity trading, specifically for LNG, crude oil and power. Our clients include some of the world's largest energy and trading companies. Our technology not only enables smarter trading decisions but also reduces CO2 emissions equivalent to what 330 million trees absorb annually. We are about to expand to singapore, houston and other places and growing fast.

The Pitch:

You spent years mastering complex problems. Now you want to build things that actually ship. We are looking for sharp quantitative minds from academia who want to become software developers. You don't need to know how to code. We will teach you full-stack development from scratch, working on production software used by some of the biggest names in global energy. What you bring is what we can't teach: the ability to think deeply, learn fast, and solve hard problems.
